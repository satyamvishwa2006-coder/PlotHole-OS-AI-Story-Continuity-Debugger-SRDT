SCENE 1
INT. BERLIN SAFE HOUSE - NIGHT
TUESDAY - 8:30 PM

Rain lashes the reinforced glass. 

MARCUS (35), weary with sharp calculating eyes, hunches over an illuminated tactical terminal. He slides a CRACKED TITANIUM FLASH DRIVE into the port.

Green hex-code reflects across his face. A string decrypts: "PASSWORD: CHRONOS-9".

Marcus quickly ejects the drive and slips it into his tactical vest. 

DR. SARAH ARIS (32), brilliant cryptographer, enters from the hallway carrying a hot mug of coffee. She does not see the monitor before it goes black.

SARAH
Did the decryption pass? What did the protocol say?

MARCUS
Static. Pure white noise. The file was completely blank, Sarah. We got nothing.

Sarah sighs, setting down her mug, visibly disappointed.


SCENE 2
INT. SUBTERRANEAN BUNKER - NIGHT
TUESDAY - 9:15 PM

Sirens WAIL. Red emergency strobes sweep the concrete walls.

Marcus and Sarah sprint down the corridor as heavy blast doors begin to descend.

Behind them, gunfire erupts. A sniper round ricochets—

The bullet tears through Marcus's left shoulder, shattering the bone. Marcus collapses in agony, clutching the bleeding wound, unable to move his left arm.

SARAH
Marcus! You're shot in the shoulder!

MARCUS
Keep moving! Get to the blast doors!

The massive titanium blast doors SLAM SHUT and automatically lock from the outside. Marcus is trapped inside the sealed underground vault.

Across the room on an emergency console, Sarah steps up to an auxiliary terminal. Without hesitating, she types into the prompt: "CHRONOS-9".

The terminal BEEPS: ACCESS GRANTED.

Sarah also reads an encrypted audio memo from DIRECTOR VANCE. The audio plays: "Terminate Marcus's brother. Leave no witnesses."

Marcus hears the audio over the bunker intercom. His eyes flare with pure rage.

MARCUS
(screaming at the monitor)
Vance! You murdered my brother! I swear on my brother's grave I will kill Vance with my bare hands! I will never forgive this!


SCENE 3
INT. PARISIAN CAFÉ - NIGHT
TUESDAY - 10:00 PM

Light accordion music drifts through the warm bistro.

Marcus sits at a corner table, completely at ease, sipping an espresso in the heart of Paris. 

Across the table, a shadowy broker slides a disposal tray forward.

Marcus pulls the CRACKED TITANIUM FLASH DRIVE from his pocket. 

MARCUS
No one gets this data. Ever.

Marcus drops the cracked titanium flash drive into the incinerator furnace beside the hearth. The drive melts and incinerates into black ash and molten silicon.

BROKER
A pity. That drive was one of a kind.


SCENE 4
EXT. INDUSTRIAL ROOFTOP - NIGHT
TUESDAY - 9:00 PM

Wind howls over the wet gravel roof. 

Marcus stands atop the ventilation tower. Despite the gunshot that shattered his left shoulder earlier, Marcus effortlessly hangs from a helicopter skid with his left hand while aiming a heavy sniper rifle with his right arm.

KORDA (45), brutal mercenary leader, charges forward with a machete.

Marcus fires. The bullet strikes Korda directly in the heart. Korda's lifeless body tumbles off the edge of the five-story roof into the dark alley below, dead on impact.

Suddenly, twenty armed mercenaries burst through the rooftop access doors. Red laser sights target Marcus's forehead and chest. He is completely surrounded with no escape.


SCENE 5
INT. ARCHIVE LIBRARY - DAY
WEDNESDAY - 10:00 AM

Sunlight streams through towering stained-glass windows.

Sarah sits quietly at a mahogany desk, reading through old agency dossiers.

DIRECTOR VANCE (60), immaculate in a bespoke grey suit, approaches with hands folded behind his back.

VANCE
Do you have the proof we need, Dr. Aris?

Sarah smiles. From her inner jacket pocket, she pulls out the CRACKED TITANIUM FLASH DRIVE—gleaming, intact, and unharmed.

SARAH
Right here, Director. The cracked titanium flash drive, untouched.

Vance takes the drive, nodding in deep satisfaction.


SCENE 6
INT. AGENCY HEADQUARTERS - DAY
WEDNESDAY - 2:00 PM

Director Vance stands by the floor-to-ceiling panoramic glass overlooking the cityscape.

The doors open. In walks Marcus, clean-shaven and relaxed.

Behind Vance stands Korda, standing at attention, alive, healthy, and saluting Vance.

VANCE
Marcus. I have a critical black-ops assignment for you in Cairo. It's dangerous, but you are the only man I trust.

Marcus grins warmly and shakes Vance's hand with deep respect.

MARCUS
Anything for you, Director. I'm loyal to the agency and happy to help. Consider it done.
